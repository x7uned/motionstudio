# Hook Archetype Library

Use these as the 10 angles when generating hooks. One archetype per hook. Adapt the wording to the user's real voice (from voice.md) and the specific topic.

---

### 1. Raw energy / reaction
They just saw something and hit record. No setup.
- "Holy crap this just worked. Look at this."
- "Okay this is actually insane."

### 2. Contrarian / pattern interrupt
Says the opposite of what everyone expects.
- "Stop posting every day. Seriously."
- "AI is making your content worse, not better."

### 3. Forbidden / secret
Implies the viewer is getting access to something they shouldn't.
- "I probably shouldn't show you this."
- "Nobody in my niche wants you to know this."

### 4. Specific numbers
Concrete before/after with real amounts.
- "I replaced a $2,000 tool with a free one."
- "This took my reply rate from 4% to 22%."

### 5. Replace + proof
Names the thing being replaced and confirms it worked.
- "Can AI replace a $5K editor? I tried it. It worked."

### 6. Curiosity gap
Reveals something exists without saying what it is.
- "There's one setting nobody's talking about."
- "This free tool is a complete cheat code."

### 7. Urgency / FOMO
A window is closing.
- "Use this before they start charging for it."
- "This loophole won't last."

### 8. Viewer callout
Directly challenges the viewer's current behavior.
- "And you're STILL doing this by hand?"
- "If you're using AI like this, stop."

### 9. Absurd escalation
Takes a real point and exaggerates for impact.
- "This will eat your whole job, not just the boring part."

### 10. Identity qualifier
Self-selects the audience in the first few words.
- "If you run a small business and hate tech, watch this."
- "For anyone who thinks AI is 'not for them' ..."

---

## Extra archetypes to rotate in

- **Tool discovery** — "Let me show you the tool I can't live without."
- **Pain agitation** — "Here's the annoying task AI now does for me."
- **Result lead** — "I made this in 4 minutes. Here's how."
- **Bold claim** — "[Tool] just killed [profession]."
- **Question hook** — "Why is nobody using this yet?"

## Rules for every hook
- 3 to 12 words. Spoken in under 3 seconds.
- Lead with the most interesting word, not a warm-up.
- It should sound like the camera turned on mid-thought.
- Benefit or curiosity in the first 3 words.
