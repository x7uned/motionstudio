## Step 0 — One-time voice setup

Check for a saved voice file at `~/Documents/script-lite/voice.md`.

**If it exists:** read it and use it as the voice calibration for everything below.

**If it does NOT exist**, run this quick setup once:

Ask the user, one at a time:
1. "What's your niche? Who do you help and with what? One or two sentences."
2. "Who's the ONE person you're talking to in every video? Their situation and biggest struggle."
3. "Paste 2 or 3 things you've actually written or said. Captions, a transcript of one of your videos, even voice-note text. Anything in your real voice. This is what I calibrate to." (If they have nothing, ask them to describe their on-camera vibe instead: casual/raw, high-energy, calm/authoritative, funny/loose, or teaching mode.)

From their answers, write `~/Documents/script-lite/voice.md` containing:
- Their niche and avatar
- A short voice profile pulled from the samples (sentence length, filler words, slang, swearing level, how they address the viewer, energy)
- A 5-row voice comparison table: "They actually say ✅ | AI would write ❌" using their real phrasing

Confirm it back briefly, then continue. They can edit this file anytime.

## Step 1 — Ask about the video

1. "What's the video about? A topic, angle, or rough idea is fine."
2. "Hot take (talking head, ~90 words) or demo/walkthrough (showing something, ~150-180 words)?"

## Step 2 — Generate 10 hook options (most important step)

**Spend most of your effort here.** The hook is the whole video. Read `hooks.md` (bundled with this skill) for the archetype library.

Generate **10 hooks**, each a DIFFERENT archetype. Each hook:
- 3 to 12 words, spoken in under 3 seconds
- Triggers instant curiosity or promises a clear benefit
- Sounds like THIS user's voice (match the samples in voice.md), not polished copy

Present them numbered with the archetype labeled:

> **1. [Raw Energy]** "Holy crap this just worked. Look."
> **2. [Cost Savings]** "I replaced a $2K tool with a free one."
> **3. [Contrarian]** "Stop using ChatGPT like that. Seriously."

After the 10, recommend your top 3 and why they'll stop the scroll for this user's audience. Ask them to pick one (or combine) before you write the script.

## Step 3 — Write the full script

For the chosen hook, include a **[VISUAL]** direction (what's on screen + on-screen text that complements, not repeats, the spoken hook).

**Body** (2 to 4 points): feels like riffing, not presenting.
- Address the viewer directly at least once ("and you're still doing this manually?")
- One hyperbolic comparison or real consequence
- Anchor to a real proof point if they have one
- Show WHAT it does / the result, not a full how-to tutorial

**CTA:** match how they actually end videos. Default to "Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send it to you."

**Caption:** a short 3 to 10 word vibe, not a summary. ("class is in session", "it's over for y'all", "thoughts?")

## Step 4 — De-AI checklist (run before delivering)

Rewrite until ALL pass:
1. Would they actually say this on camera? If it reads written, rewrite it spoken.
2. No clean transitions. Cut "Additionally", "Furthermore", "Here's the thing", "Now", "Moreover".
3. Break parallel structure and rule-of-three stacking. Real speech is messier.
4. 2 to 3 natural filler words ("like", "literally", "for real", "right?").
5. 2 to 3 sentence fragments. Not every thought is a full sentence.
6. No sentence over 25 words.
7. Address the viewer at least once.
8. Banned AI tells: "What if I told you", "The truth is", "Let that sink in", "Here's the kicker", "Let me break this down", "In other words", "delve", "tapestry", "underscore".
9. No em dashes. Use periods, commas, or line breaks.

## Step 5 — Deliver

Present ONE clean final script: hook, [VISUAL], body, CTA, caption. Don't show drafts.

Then ask: "Want me to punch up the hook, change the angle, or write a variation?"
