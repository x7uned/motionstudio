In Pac-Man, only the red ghost is really chasing you. The other three follow their own rules. Every ghost aims at one target square. At each turn, it picks the way that looks closest to that square, and it never turns back. Red's target is you. Pink aims four squares in front of you, so she tries to cut you off. Blue is the strangest. He takes the spot two squares ahead of you, draws a line from Red to it, and doubles it. So Red and Blue often close in from both sides. Orange chases you only from far away. When he gets within eight squares, he turns and heads for his corner. Why not let all four chase you? Toru Iwatani, who designed the game, said four ghosts in one line would be boring.
