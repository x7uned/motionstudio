## Art direction (the point of v2)
Reference for quality: the casino series (`casino-tables/film/qa/contact-sheet.jpg`, `casino-floor`), `cat-zoomies` apartment, Pac-Man films. Rich, warm, every frame full and lit, fine 32-bit-style pixels, dark 1-px outlines, volume by tone steps + Bayer dither, many depth layers with parallax.

1. **The whole 1080×1920 frame is designed.** v1 had the street in the top ~60% and a dark empty road band with sliding cars below. Instead: a European city street in late-afternoon sun, seen from slightly above (3/4 high angle) so the frame fills top to bottom: sky and distant skyline (where the Eiffel silhouettes and gold piles will rise) → building facades with detail (cornices, balconies, shop signs with no brands, awnings, lamp posts, wires, pigeons on ledges) → wide sidewalk where the action happens (main safe zone y300–1250) → curb, road with markings, a crosswalk, manhole, puddles, parked bikes in the lower part, lit, readable, never a black void. Long warm shadows, slight haze on far layers.
2. **People are ours, adult, to scale.** Passers-by drawn new in the film's style with real adult proportions (1.6–1.85 m next to Steve's 1.8 m), varied clothes and ages, a few with phones filming Steve, a coffee cup that drops, a cyclist, a bus at the stop. Reactions animate smoothly. No chibi kids from v1.
3. **Steve** keeps the v1 design and blocky motion; he must read as the brightest, most saturated object.
4. **Scale that sells the numbers:** 1 m block = Steve's waist-to-head height ratio right (Steve 1.8 m). African elephants ≈3 m at the shoulder, so three elephants tower over the 1 m gold block on a big platform scale. The strongman is a real-sized human. Eiffel silhouettes are huge on the horizon. The 5½ gold mountains are big shapes in the sky/skyline, clearly labelled.
5. **Bank, tree, concrete barrier, scales, shulker box, inventory grid, counters, speedometer, portal, cracks** — all drawn new for this film (you may reuse v1's own new modules — portal, break-cracks, item-drop, gold-block, concrete-block, log, shulker-box, inventory-grid, elephant, strongman, eiffel-silhouette, gold-mountain, speedometer, timer, asphalt-cracks — only if they are NOT built on catalog kits; otherwise redraw them, and in any case raise them to the new art level).
6. **Numbers:** the thousands separator must read as a COMMA in every font size (a tail below the baseline): «2,304», «44,000 t», «1,200,000 t», «220,000 t». v1's big counter showed «44.000» — fix. Counters roll smoothly and land on the word.
7. Keep the shot table timing; the camera may move more (pull back for the skyline, track Steve's run, push in on the final step).
