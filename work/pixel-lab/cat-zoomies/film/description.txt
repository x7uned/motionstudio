Why does your cat sleep all day, then go crazy at 3 a.m.?

A cat sleeps about 15 hours a day, much of it light sleep. It's built to hunt: in the wild it catches about ten mice a day, each one a long wait and a short sprint, and it rests in between to save energy. A bowl means no hunt, so the energy comes out at night. Vets call it FRAPs; you call it the zoomies. The fix: play until it's tired, then feed it.
