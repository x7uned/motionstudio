Pac-Man's red ghost gets faster when you're almost done. On level one, you run at 80 percent of top speed. Ghosts run at 75, so you're a bit faster. But every dot you eat stops you for one frame, a sixtieth of a second. In a hall full of dots, you drop to about 71, and a ghost behind you slowly catches up. Now watch the dots left. At 20, the red ghost, Blinky, speeds up to 80. That's your speed. And he stops taking breaks. When the other ghosts run to their corners, he keeps chasing you. At 10 dots, he goes to 85.
