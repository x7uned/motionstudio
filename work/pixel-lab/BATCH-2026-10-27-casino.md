## Series look (the user asked for good drawing: this is the priority)
- **One casino for all five films.** Film 1 builds the shared casino kit in `casino-math/film/` (`casino.mjs` and helpers: hall, lamps, carpet, slot rows, roulette table, people, chips, dealer). Films 2–5 copy that kit as a frozen copy into their own `film/` and extend it; never edit an earlier film's folder.
- **Art direction:** a warm, rich casino interior at night. Deep red patterned carpet, dark wood and brass, green felt, pools of warm lamp light over tables, glowing slot rows in the back with soft bloom (dithered glow), little bokeh lights, silhouetted guests in the depth layers. Many depth layers (back wall → slot rows → tables → foreground rail/chairs), parallax on camera moves. Detailed props: chips with edge stripes, roulette wheel with frets and a moving ball, cards with readable pips, reels with symbols. Every frame full and lit; avoid long dark or tiny close-ups (lesson from crt). Fine 32-bit pixels, dark 1-px outlines, volume by steps + Bayer dither.
- **"You":** the recurring hero in the coral puffer, purple bobble hat, blue scarf, yellow mittens (`touch/film/hero.mjs`, read-only copy). Dealers, waitress, big player — our own characters in the same style, no real faces.
- **No real brands:** no casino names or logos, no slot-maker marks. Generic symbols only (cherry, bell, BAR, 7). On-screen words: few, our pixel fonts, Latin only.
- **Numbers on screen** only where the script table names them; the voice keeps numbers minimal (user's wish).
- **Series card:** cover kicker «CASINO SECRETS» + a small «1/5 … 5/5» chip.
- **Sound:** synthesized 8-bit: ball rattle, wheel tick, chip clacks, card swish, reel spin and stop clunks, win jingle (the fake win in film 2 plays a real-sounding jingle), dice rattle; casino-lounge chiptune bed (calm, jazzy) about −12 dB under speech, cadence at the end.

## Film-specific notes
1. **casino-math:** harness = frozen copy of `pal-50hz/film` (render/captions, mix, synth-core, sheet, cover, cta, cute, tts.py, dev/qa.mjs, dev/pops.mjs). Roulette table hero shot (three-quarter top view so the wheel reads as a wheel). Wheel: 37 pockets, one green 0; the ball as a pure function of t (spins down, bounces over frets, settles on the word). «millions of games»: smooth pull-back over the whole floor, many wheels and slots active. Line chart «YOU» zigzag vs «CASINO» straight up. Doubling: chip tower 1·2·4·8·16… grows on each loss and wobbles at the top of the safe zone. Calendar: a night and a month turn red, the year stays green.
2. **casino-slots:** a slot machine big, then a cut-away (casing dissolves to show a chip with running numbers). Result «LOCKED» at the press, reels just a show. Near miss: the 7 stops just above the payline. Virtual reel ribbon. Fake win: $1 in, 50¢ out, WIN lights. Two bars WITH SOUND / REAL (no percentages). STOP button changes nothing.
3. **casino-floor:** isometric cut-away of the whole casino (dollhouse, no roof), small hero walking a dotted path through a maze of slot rows; MYTH stamps on clocks and O₂; scent swirls from vents; music notes speed up; then a bright «new» casino grows next to it (high ceiling, skylights, palms, clocks); the sun sets while the hero keeps playing; dotted cage outline at «The trap just got nicer».
4. **casino-money:** cashier cage (cash → chips), waitress with a FREE drink tag that flips, player card with a gift ribbon → three icons ($, ⚡, ⏱) → EXPECTED LOSS board → a slice becomes dinner/room key/ticket; gifts upgrade; big player with a REBATE envelope; NO FREE DRINKS sign.
5. **casino-tables:** blackjack (both 22, you lose; order 1 YOU → 2 DEALER), table sign 6 TO 5 vs 3 TO 2 and two stacks, roulette last-numbers board with five reds, dice thrown hard, macro of a clear red casino die with a serial number, dealer's open palms, ceiling camera domes with red lights.
