How does a cat drink without scooping a single drop?

Its tongue barely touches the water. The curled tip pulls up a thin column, and the cat shuts its mouth just before gravity pulls it back, about four times a second. Lions and tigers do the same, only slower. Dogs use the same trick, but hit the water much harder.
