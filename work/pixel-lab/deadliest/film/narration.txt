Which animal kills the most people? The shark? No! The shark is the safest one on this list. Sharks kill about ten people a year. Lions kill about a hundred. A hippo looks lazy, but it kills about five hundred. It attacks boats that come too close. Crocodiles kill up to a thousand. They wait under the water, right by the shore. A scorpion is tiny. Still, its sting kills about three thousand people a year, mostly children. Dogs kill about sixty thousand. Not with their teeth, but with rabies, a virus in the bite. A vaccine stops it. Snakes kill about a hundred thousand people every year. So which animal is number one? A great white shark? A hungry lion? A giant snake? Nope. It's this little guy: the mosquito. Yes.
