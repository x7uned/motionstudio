This ant is a zombie, and the fungus inside it never touches its brain. It starts with one spore, a tiny fungus seed. It lands on an ant in the rainforest and grows through its shell. For days, the ant acts normal. Then it leaves its nest high in the trees, shakes, and falls to the forest floor. It climbs a small plant, about twenty-five centimeters up, where the air is warm and wet. Right around noon, it bites the bottom of a leaf and never lets go. Inside, the fungus has wrapped the muscles all over its body. In 2017, scientists looked closer: fungus everywhere, but not in the brain. How it controls the ant is still a mystery. The ant dies. A few days later, a stalk grows out of its head and rains spores on the path below. Bite marks like these were found on fossil leaves forty-eight million years old.
