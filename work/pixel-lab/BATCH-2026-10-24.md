## Format for every film (same as the hit)
- Account Kagenami (@kagenami_ai), English. Voice Dennis · Eleven v4, `language_code=en`, the way the existing films do it (`tts.py` in `pacman/film` / `cat-blink/film`). Plain English (AGENTS.md): simple words, short sentences, a technical term explained at once.
- **Hook:** the first sentence is the claim itself, under ~10 words, with the topic word. Never «Have you ever wondered / Did you know / Imagine». The hero is already on screen and moving at frame 0.
- **Length:** about 45–60 s (≈140–175 words). One plain paragraph, one connected causal story, not a list of questions.
- **Ending: loop** (the hit's ending). The last line runs on into the first words, the camera lands on the exact first frame, the seam pause matches a sentence pause (~0.4 s), music cycles through the seam with no fade, and every repeating motion has whole cycles over END. Not the share/follow card and not the «Follow …» outro. Do not make «two loops in a row» review copies.
- **Picture:** «Пиксель-фильм» style (docs/pixel-film.md), 1080×1920 9:16, the picture fills the frame edge to edge, **no overlay** (no avatar, handle, title, duration, chapter bars). Only karaoke captions at the bottom on a soft dithered shade.
- **Motion:** nothing appears, disappears or changes in one frame. Pose, palette, flash and segment changes cross-fade (scan with a pops check like `tetris/film/dev/pops.mjs` before the full render).
- **Sound:** only synthesized 8-bit sounds, each lasting exactly its motion; chiptune bed about −12 dB under speech; no original game sounds or music.
- **Games:** our own pixel drawings in the style of the game: no ripped sprites, ROM graphics, logos or original maps. Pac-Man films may reuse `pacman/film` (maze.mjs, sprites.mjs, sim.mjs with the Dossier ghost rules, dev/ai.mjs, dev/search3.mjs).
- **Facts:** research every claim before the script, write `facts.md` with links (subagents may do the research), and keep honest caveats. If a claim turns out wrong or too shaky, change the angle and say so in the README. Never invent numbers.

## Paid voice
Narration is authorized by the user's order. One TTS take per film. At most 2 re-takes per film, and only for a broken take (wrong word, failed alignment, mispronounced topic word); no voice auditions or variant takes.

## Shared files and the render slot
- Work only in your own film folders `work/pixel-lab/<film>/`. Do not edit other films' folders, `work/pixel-lab/shared/` or `renderer/`. Copy what you need into your folder (frozen copies), so finished films stay byte-identical.
- Every heavy job (a full `render.mjs --video`, the mix, the phone/max encode) goes through `work/pixel-lab/render-lock.sh <label> <cmd…>`. Three sessions share one slot. Stills and contact sheets don't need it.
- Do not start the studio engine or the Next.js UI; these films are built in the pixel-lab harness.

## Done = per film
- `film/<name>-pixel.mp4` master, a send copy ≤30 MiB (`-phone.mp4`; or `-max.mp4`, 2-pass libx264 slow ~28.6 MiB with the master audio copied), `cover.png` (cover margins: kicker y300, title ≤y590, scene y590–1490, avatar + handle + chip in one row at y1545), `description.txt` (≈10 words with the topic word, then an emoji, up to 4 hashtags, then a `Sources:` line with names), `credits.txt`, `script.md`, `facts.md`, `README.md`.
- QA from the final MP4: contact sheet, seam check, a pops scan, and a real playback of the exported file (static frames are not a playback test).
- **Send it right away**, before starting the next film: SendUserFile (status proactive, display attach) with the send copy, cover.png and description.txt. Caption: title · Kagenami · duration · folder · «не одобрен». Then go on.
- Nothing is approved until the user says so.
- At the end, write a 3-line status per film in this file under «Status».
