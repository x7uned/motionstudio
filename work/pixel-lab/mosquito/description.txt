Why do mosquitoes always bite you first?

Only females bite: they need blood to make eggs. They choose you before they taste you. Your breath, the red and orange of your skin and your body heat bring them close. The final choice is smell: in a lab test, the favorite had much more of three fatty acids on the skin, and was still the favorite three years later.
