Sources (details and links in ../facts.md):
- Honeybee ~0.1 g; nectar loads ~40% of body mass: Feuerbacher et al., J Exp Biol 2003.
- Bee hummingbird ~1.6 g, often mistaken for a bee: Guinness World Records; Audubon.
- House mouse 12–30 g: Animal Diversity Web. Falling mouse: J.B.S. Haldane, "On Being the Right Size", 1926 (a thought example).
- Domestic cat ~4 kg: Kienzle & Moik, Br J Nutr 2011; righting reflex: Scientific American.
- All dogs weigh about as much as all wild land mammals; humans ~20× more: Greenspoon et al., PNAS 2023 (Weizmann Institute).
- Average adult human 62 kg: Walpole et al., BMC Public Health 2012.
- Horse ~500 kg, a single toe per leg: PetMD (University of Minnesota data); McHorse et al., Proc R Soc B 2017.
- Hippo can't swim or float, walks the river bottom: San Diego Zoo; National Geographic.
- African elephant ~6 t, up to 150 kg of plants a day: San Diego Zoo; National Geographic.
- Blue whale up to ~150 t (NOAA Fisheries); heart ~180–200 kg (National Geographic Education; Guinness).
