Your cat says "I love you" with its eyes, and most people miss it. Watch a cat that trusts you. It looks at you, then slowly narrows its eyes and closes them for a moment. That's a slow blink. For cats, a long, hard stare can feel like a threat. Closing their eyes in front of you means: I feel safe with you. In 2020, scientists in the UK tested it. Owners slow-blinked at their cats, and the cats blinked back. Then a stranger tried it. The cats blinked back at the stranger too, and they were more likely to walk up to the stranger's hand. Want to try? Look at your cat with soft eyes, like a relaxed smile. Slowly close your eyes for a second or two, then open them. If your cat blinks back, it just said it too.
