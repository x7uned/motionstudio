How do you say "I love you" to your cat?

Slowly blink at it. For cats, a long hard stare can feel like a threat, so closing their eyes in front of you means they feel safe. In a 2020 UK study, cats blinked back at owners who slow-blinked, and even walked up to strangers who did it. Try it: soft eyes, close them for a second or two, open.
