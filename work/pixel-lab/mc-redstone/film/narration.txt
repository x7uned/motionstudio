People have built real computers inside Minecraft, out of red dust. They work on the same idea as the chip in your phone. The dust is called redstone, and it works like a wire. Flip a lever, and power runs down the dust and lights a lamp. On or off: that's all a computer needs. A redstone torch does the opposite. Power its block, and the torch goes dark. That's a NOT gate. Three torches make an AND gate: the lamp lights only when both levers are on. Two wires that join make an OR gate: either lever works. Put a few gates together, and they can add. One plus one gives one, zero: that's two in computer numbers. Copy that thousands of times, and you get a whole computer. In 2021, a player called sammyuri built one that plays Tetris. But it ticks once a second. A phone chip ticks about four billion times. In 2025, sammyuri built a tiny chatbot from redstone. On its own, one answer would take about ten years.
