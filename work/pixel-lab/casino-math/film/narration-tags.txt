A casino doesn't need to cheat you. One little green number does all the work. A roulette wheel has thirty-seven numbers. But when you win, it pays you as if there were only thirty-six. That green zero belongs to the casino. It's a tiny gap, just a few cents from every bet. Tonight, you can still win. Someone at the table usually does. But the casino isn't playing one game. It plays millions of games, every day. Over that many games, luck evens out, and only the tiny gap is left. Your line jumps up and down. The casino's line just goes up. Doubling your bet after a loss doesn't save you. Lose a few times in a row, and you're betting a fortune to win back one chip. A casino can lose a night, even a month. It almost never loses a year.
