Minecraft has removed Herobrine more than thirty times. He was never in the game. In 2010, an anonymous story went around 4chan and the Minecraft forums. A player was alone in a new world, in thick fog. Far away stood a figure that looked like the normal player, but with empty white eyes. When the player walked closer, it ran into the fog. The player's forum posts kept disappearing. Then a message came from someone called Herobrine. One word: Stop. The story also listed his signs: two-by-two tunnels, sand pyramids in the sea, trees with no leaves. That same month, a streamer showed Herobrine live, screamed, and ended the stream. It was staged, with a picture of Herobrine hidden in a house. The story said Herobrine was the dead brother of Notch, who made Minecraft. Notch said there was no dead brother, and Herobrine was never in the game. So Mojang turned it into a joke. In 2011, the update notes said: Removed Herobrine.
