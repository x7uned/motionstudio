Pac-Man's ghost names got lost in translation. You know them as Blinky, Pinky, Inky and Clyde. The arcade screen also describes each one in a single word: Shadow, Speedy, Bashful and Pokey. But Speedy is no faster than the others, and Pokey is no slower. In the Japanese original, the words tell you how each ghost really hunts. Blinky is oikake, the chaser. He aims right at your square. Pinky is machibuse, the ambusher, aiming four squares ahead of you. Inky is kimagure, moody. His target depends on where Blinky is, so his path keeps changing. And Clyde is otoboke, playing dumb. From far away, he chases you. But closer than eight squares, he turns and heads for his corner. So he keeps circling you. Does Clyde do nothing, then? His corner is the bottom left.
