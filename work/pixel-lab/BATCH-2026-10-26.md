## Save the user's limits (the user asked for this explicitly)
- **Build alone. Do not spawn subagents.**
- **Reuse code before writing anything new.** Copy the closest earlier film harness into `<folder>/film/` as a frozen copy and adapt it. The harness has timeline/render/sheet/synth/mix/cover/tts/qa. Never edit other films' folders, `shared/` or `renderer/`.
  - Good sources: `ant-zombie/film` (newest side-view harness, QA scripts `dev/pops.mjs`, `dev/qa.mjs`), `mc-redstone/film` and `mc-craftgpt/film` (blocky Minecraft world), `deadliest/film` (follow card `levels/cta.mjs`, cute kit, hero use), `cat-zoomies/film` (apartment, cat, `person.mjs`), `animals-wingbeat/film` (sound shown as waves/notes), `pacman*/film` (arcade look), `shared/` (hero.mjs, synth-core.mjs, scribe.py, retime.mjs, BRIEF-COMMON.md).
- Don't read large files whole when a grep or a head answers the question. Use few contact sheets: one draft sheet, one fix pass, one final sheet. Don't polish endlessly; ship a clean, readable film.

## Format (same for every film)
- **Account and voice:** Kagenami (@kagenami_ai), English, Plain English. Voice Dennis · Eleven v4 `[thoughtful]`, `language_code=en`, via the cached `tts.py` pattern from `ant-zombie/film/tts.py`. The key is read from `.env.local` inside the process only; never print, copy or log it.
  - Paid voice is authorized by the user's order: **one take per film**.
  - At most 2 re-takes, only for a broken take (wrong or missing word, failed alignment, mispronounced topic word).
  - No auditions, no variant takes.
  - Word timings from the TTS alignment (and `shared/scribe.py` if needed).
- **Text:** the narration is the `> ` paragraph in the film's `script.md`. Markers in square brackets `[…]` are sound cues, not words; don't send them to TTS. Write numbers the way they should be spoken (the script already does). Keep facts exactly as written. Wording may change only for TTS problems; if it does, note why in README.
- **Ending: NOT a loop** (user decision 2026-10-26). The last line is the follow ask from the script. On «Follow…» a FOLLOW → FOLLOWING ✓ card with a bell on «miss» slides in smoothly over the final scene, with no scene change: copy the button part of `deadliest/film/levels/cta.mjs`, without «LIKED IT? / SEND». No seam or first-frame matching is needed. Hold the last frame ~0.8 s after the voice ends, with the music finishing on a cadence (no abrupt cut).
- **Hook:** the hero object is on screen and already moving at frame 0, entering within 0.5 s.
- **Picture:** «Пиксель-фильм» (docs/pixel-film.md, shared/BRIEF-COMMON.md).
  - 540×960 grid ×2 → 1080×1920, 30 fps.
  - One persistent world per film; things change on narration cues.
  - No overlay (no avatar, handle, title, duration, chapter bars): the picture runs to every edge.
  - Karaoke captions in the row y≈690–711 (grid) on a soft dithered shade.
  - Safe zones v1: key content x 35–440 (to x 505 above y 400), y 150–625 on the grid; check key stills with `scripts/safe-zones-overlay.mjs`.
- **Motion:** nothing appears, disappears or changes in one frame. Use dithered dissolves, eased motion and crossfaded frames. Pure functions of t, no Math.random.
- **Props:** our own drawings. No brand logos on props, no ripped game sprites, no real faces. On-screen labels: few words, our pixel fonts, Latin only.
- **Sound:**
  - Synthesized 8-bit sounds only (exception: film 2, see below), each lasting exactly its motion.
  - Chiptune bed about −12 dB under speech.
  - Voice about −16 LUFS integrated.
  - No original game music or sound effects.

## Render, QA, deliverables
- Every heavy job (full `render.mjs --video`, mix, max/phone encode) goes through `work/pixel-lab/render-lock.sh <label> <cmd…>`. Stills and sheets don't need it. Don't start the studio engine or Next.js.
- **QA from the final MP4:**
  - frame/duration check;
  - pops scan (no one-frame flashes);
  - loudness;
  - a 30-frame contact sheet `qa/contact-sheet.jpg`;
  - safe-zone overlay on ~8 key frames;
  - a real playback of the exported file in the built-in browser if you can. If you can't, say so: static frames are not a playback test.
- **Deliverables in `<folder>/film/`:**
  - `<name>-pixel.mp4` master;
  - a send copy ≤ 30 MiB (`-max.mp4`, 2-pass libx264 slow ≈ 28.6 MiB with the master audio copied, or the master itself if it is ≤ 30 MiB);
  - `cover.png`, using the cover margins: kicker y300, pixel title ≤ y590, scene y590–1490, avatar + handle + chip in one row at y1545. Copy an earlier film's `cover.mjs`;
  - `description.txt`: about 10 words with the topic word, then an emoji, up to 4 hashtags (1–2 broad + 2–3 topic, no brand tags, no #fyp/#viral), then a `Sources:` line with names;
  - `credits.txt`, `facts.md` (copy the facts and links from script.md), `README.md` (what was built, QA numbers, what was not checked).
- **Do not send files to the user.** The lead checks and sends them. Nothing is approved until the user says so.
- **Report back in ≤ 250 words:** paths, duration, word count, TTS takes used, QA numbers, known issues.

## Film-specific notes
1. **rps:**
   - Two players in one persistent schoolyard or classroom. The channel's hero (`shared/hero.mjs`) can be one player and the human hero from `cat-zoomies/film/person.mjs` the other.

2. **mc-ghast:**
   - Blocky world (copy the mc-redstone engine): the Nether (lava, netherrack, our own blocky ghast) and a small 2010 home studio through a portal, with a cat by a microphone.
   - Sound lab panel with waveform + spectrogram.
   - Demos 1–3 are OUR synthesized imitation (formant-synth meow 0.84 s, F0 arch ≈600→880→500 Hz, F1≈1460, F2≈3050; half-speed resample 1.7 s; long reverb), labelled «OUR COPY».
   - Then the REAL ghast from `mc-ghast/media/mojang/` (user-approved exception; see SOURCE.md): moan3 as is, then moan1 three times at pitch 0.8 / 1.0 / 1.2 by resampling. Label «REAL GHAST · MINECRAFT». Credits «Ghast sounds © Mojang Studios».
   - Leave ~1 s gaps in the voice for each demo if the take allows. Otherwise play demos under the voice about −4 dB, as in animals-wingbeat. Check whether the TTS model honours pauses.
   - TTS: write «C418» as «C-four-eighteen» for the voice, «C418» in captions.
3. **card-shuffle:**
   - A green table and our pixel hands; riffle, overhand and perfect faro shuffles animated on real card order.
   - The 68-digit number as a ticker.
   - Pixel Earth: reuse `renderer/pixel/earth.mjs` read-only.
   - The «how far from random» bars use 1.00, 1.00, 1.00, 1.00, 0.92, 0.61, 0.33.
   - After 8 perfect out-shuffles the spread shows the starting order again. Simulate it in code so it is exact.
4. **claw-machine:**
   - One cut-away claw machine: service panel with the settings screen, play counter, claw power gauge (48 V / 10 V), and the grip fading during the lift.
   - Own plush toys only.
   - Manual pages are our drawings with paraphrased lines.
5. **doom-ranking:**
