Is a Minecraft world really endless? Most of it doesn't exist until you get close.

The game builds the world in chunks as you move, all from one number, the seed. Noise picks land, ocean, mountains and biomes; 3D noise carves caves. The edge is a wall 30 million blocks from the center. Old versions broke at 12.5 million, and one streamer walked there for 14 years.
