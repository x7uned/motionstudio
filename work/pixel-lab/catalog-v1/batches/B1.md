# Catalog v1 · batch B1 (2026-10-26)

Ten items ported, none skipped. Checks are the last `node tools/sheet.mjs <kind>` run. Every remaining flag was looked
at frame by frame: they are the engine's own dissolve levels or 1-px re-rasterisation under the drifting camera, not
one-frame changes (see "Shared changes I would propose"). "Real draw" = the item's draw call timed on its own. The
sheet's ms/frame figure also includes harness overhead, about 15–25 ms.

## Shared changes I would propose
1. **tools/sheet.mjs, item check.** Skip the frames of the engine's own enter/exit dissolve. Their first or last Bayer level
   counts as "novel" whenever the item is otherwise still: dog at 8.47 s, wolf at 0.67 s and arrow at 5.57 s are only that.
   The cat probably passes because its tail and breathing keep the neighbouring frames busy.
2. **tools/sheet.mjs, backdrop check.** Under the drift (1 + 0.0045 t) a horizontal ordered-dither band or a 1-px line
   re-phases a whole row at once. Some tolerance, or a check done without the drift, would separate this from real pops.
   Living-room and kitchen needed pattern fixes for it, and their last flags are this kind.
3. **engine/props.mjs.** Pass the target's presence along with its point (for example `s.lookA` 0..1, or lookAt returning
   `{ pt, a }`). Aimed items (arrow, sound-wave toward, person point, dog/cat look) could then ease their aim in while the
   target dissolves in; today they re-aim in one frame if the target enters later.
4. **engine/props.mjs.** Give the item its slot / world x (`s.slot`) so side-view characters (wolf) could face the
   centre or their target without flipping as the camera moves.
5. **sheet ms/frame** includes about 15–25 ms of harness work (frame copies, analysis); it reads high for every item.
