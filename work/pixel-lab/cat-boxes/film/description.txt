Why does your cat ignore its new bed and sit in the box?

Hiding is how cats deal with stress: in a Dutch shelter, new cats with a box calmed down in about three days, cats without one took about two weeks. Boxes are also warm: cats feel best around 30–38 °C. And a box is a hunter's hideout. Cats even sit in a square drawn on paper, and in an illusory one.
