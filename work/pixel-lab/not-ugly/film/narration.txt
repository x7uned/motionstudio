You're not ugly. Your phone is lying to you. A selfie is taken about 30 centimetres from your face. That close, the camera makes your nose look about 30% wider than from one and a half metres away. Nobody else sees you from that close. Photos also feel wrong because they aren't flipped. You see your face in the mirror every day, so you like the mirror version. In a 1977 study, people chose their mirror photo, and their friends chose the real one. And your voice? When you talk, you hear it through the air and through the bones of your head. Bones carry the low sounds, so in your head your voice is deeper. A recording only catches the air. But in one test, people rated voices without knowing theirs was among them.
