# «The lowest aliens can't hit you» · сценарий v1 (2026-10-24)

## Текст (180 слов, один абзац, один дубль)

In Space Invaders, the lowest aliens can't hit you. They still shoot, but their shots can't hurt you. Players in Japan called it the Nagoya attack. Here's the bug. Every alien shot starts a little below the alien. And the game only counts a hit in a thin strip at your cannon's height. Higher aliens drop shots into that strip, and you die. But when the army comes all the way down, right on top of you, their shots start below it. They touch your cannon, and the game says: too low. One row higher, and they can kill you again. One more step down, and the aliens land. Game over, even with lives left. So players let the army come down on purpose, and cleared the front row at point-blank range. The army also speeds up as it shrinks: the game moves just one alien per frame, so the last alien moves sixty times a second. Its creator called it a bug, and Taito left it in. So next time the army is right on top of you, remember:
