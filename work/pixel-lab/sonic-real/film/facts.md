## Facts and calculations

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Running top speed | 6 px/frame | Sonic Retro, Sonic Physics Guide (SPG: Running); the same value is quoted by other summaries |
| Acceleration | 0.046875 px/frame² (top speed in 128 frames ≈ 2.1 s) | SPG: Running |
| Rolling speed cap | 16 px/frame, in all classic games | Speed Demos Archive, Sonic 1 «Game Mechanics»; SPG |
| Rolling | No speed gain from the button; friction slows the ball; only a slope speeds it up | SPG: Rolling (the classic games' rule) |
| Standing height | 39 px (height radius 19) | SPG: Characters / Hitboxes |
| Frame rate | ≈60 fps (NTSC Mega Drive / Genesis) | — |
| Official height | 100 cm (35 kg) | Sonic Retro and fan wikis cite Sega's profile. **Not confirmed on Sega's own page**, so the text says «about one meter» |
| Usain Bolt's top speed | ≈44.7 km/h (12.4 m/s, Berlin 2009) | World Athletics biomechanics report |
| Movie «Sonic the Hedgehog» (2020) | Sheriff Tom's radar gun reads about 296 mph on the first pass, and about 300 mph later | Several matching descriptions of the scene (Crosswalk, Univision, MovieMistakes, a film fan wiki). **I have not seen the frame myself.** The text says «about 300». 300 mph = 482.8 km/h → «about 480» |
| Cartoons and comics | «faster than the speed of sound» | The common description of the character (Wikipedia, Mario Wiki and others). Not confirmed on Sega's own site, so the text says «in cartoons and comics», not «officially» |
| Speed of sound | ≈343 m/s = ≈1,235 km/h (air at 20 °C) | Physics → «over 1,200» |
| The name | Chosen because it means speed (the speed of sound) | Fan wikis quote the interview with Naoto Ohshima. In the text: «That's where his name comes from» |

Calculations (scale 39 px = 1 m → 1 px = 2.56 cm):
- running: 6 × 60 × 0.0256 = 9.2 m/s = **33 km/h**;
- rolling: 16 × 60 × 0.0256 = 24.6 m/s = **88.6 km/h**; 88.6 / 44.7 = **1.98** («twice as fast as Bolt»);
- loop 6 m tall (r = 3 m), with no friction: at the top you need v² ≥ g·r, so at the bottom v ≥ √(5·g·r) = 12.1 m/s = **43.7 km/h** («about 44»);
- at 33 km/h, Sonic does not even reach the top: v² = 85 − 2·9.81·6 < 0;
- at 88.6 km/h, the speed at the top is ≈22 m/s, against the 5.4 m/s needed: «with speed to spare».

Assumptions:
- The 6 m loop height («as tall as a two-story house») is our choice, not the size of a loop from the game. The text does not say that game loops are that size.
- Friction and the size of the ball are ignored in the loop calculation.
