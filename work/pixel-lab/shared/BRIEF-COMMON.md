# Common rules for pixel-film builders (Kagenami, 2026-10-24)

## Style («Пиксель-фильм»)
- 540×960 grid, ×2 nearest → 1080×1920, 30 fps. Flat tone bands with Bayer dithering, a dark 1-px outline on characters and props, warm and readable at phone size. Fine "32-bit" pixels, not chunky 8-bit blocks.
- Every appearance / disappearance / state change is continuous: dithered dissolve (`fade(c, a)` from raster.mjs), eased motion, crossfaded sprite frames. **Never a one-frame change** (the user's rule).
- One persistent world: things grow, change and move on narration cues; nothing "cuts" to a new page.
- Illustrations, not real products: no brand names or logos on props (no Apple/Otis/Boeing marks), no real people's faces. The lead adds the few real logos/flags as Commons tiles.
- Plain, readable labels: few words, our pixel fonts (`MAIN` 5×7, `TINY` 3×5, Latin only), scale 2 or 3.

## Files to read first
- `timeline.mjs` (the film's single source of truth: `EV` word times, layout constants, beat windows, `CAM`), `render.mjs` (the harness: sky → disc → `back` → `actors` → `front` → `hud` → overlays → caption shade → captions), `world.mjs` (`view()` → V with `V.X(wx)`, `V.Y(wy)`, `V.x0..x1`, `V.y0..y1`; `drawSky`, `drawDisc`), `cute.mjs` (kit: `blob`, `flat`, `ell`, `tube`, `bez`, `eye`/`eyes`, `blinkAt`, `blush`, `smile`, `shadow`, `sign`, `pickup`, `tag`, `panel`, `RAMP`, `C`, `sm`, `ease`, `lerp`, `win`, `rnd`, `fade`, `mix`, `bayer`, `MAIN`/`TINY`), `levels/hero.mjs` (the channel's character: `makeHero`, see below), `../../../../renderer/pixel/raster.mjs` (`Canvas`: `px rect rrect frame rframe line poly disc ring ellipse ering dither tint text measure get blit`; `fade(c, a)`, `mix`, `hex`, `bayer`).
- A module may export `RANGE = [wx0, wx1]` (culling), `fill`, `back`, `actors`, `front`, `hud` — each `(c, V, t)`.

## The channel's character (`levels/hero.mjs`)
The user's own pixel avatar (sprite sheet, cells 192×208, drawn 1:1 into the grid ≈ 190 px tall). **Never redraw it**; only pose, mirror, grade, clip. `makeHero({ poses, mirror, hops, grade, x, y })` → `{ draw(c, V, t, { clip, a }), shadow(c, V, t), box(V, t), state(t) }`.
Rows (frames): 0 idle (0–6; 2 = wink) · 1 wave (0–3) · 2 talk/explain (0–5) · 3 shrug/open hands (0–5) · 4 point up-right (0–5; fingertip ≈ cell (148, 38), i.e. +47, −163 from his feet when facing right) · 5 think, hand on chin (0–5) · 6 arms crossed (0–5) · 7 surprised, hands up (0–5) · 8 hands together at the chest (0–5) · 9 excited/happy (0–5). Poses list: `[t0, row, [frames], secondsPerFrame]`; crossfades are automatic (≥ 6 frames). He has no phone pose: draw the phone into his hands (row 8) as your own prop. Grades: `golden`, `day`, `lamp`, `cabin`, `none`.

## Layout rules (screen, 540×960 grid; safe zones v1 since 2026-10-25, docs/safe-zones.md, numbers ×0.5)
- The picture runs to every edge, but platform UI covers the edges: everything important (labels, numbers, faces/eyes, cards, the hero's landing, key action, accents) stays in the main zone x 35–440 (up to x 505 above y 400), y 150–625; best focus x 70–440, y 210–625.
- Background only (sky, floor, foreground detail; no text, faces, cards or key action; things may pass through, never stop or act there): top y 0–125 (TikTok tabs, search), bottom y 750–960 (handle, description, sound, progress bar) and the right button column x > 470 below y 400. Caution (edges of big shapes only): y 125–150, y 625–680, x 440–470 below y 400. No text within 35 px of the sides.
- Captions: text row y ≈ 690–711 inside y 680–750, white karaoke text over a soft dithered shade the harness draws (≈ 640–790). Nothing else to read there.
- The lead's cards and the share/follow card start at y ≥ 150 (older films used y ≈ 96–104, under TikTok's tabs); top y 150–330 is theirs at the times listed in your film brief. Check stills with `node ../../../../scripts/safe-zones-overlay.mjs stills/tX.png dev/<yours>-zones.png` (paths relative to the film folder).

## Rules
1. Nothing changes in one frame (see Style).
2. Pure functions of t: no `Math.random`, no wall clock; use `rnd(seed)` and only `EV` / timeline values (small offsets around words are fine).
3. Speed: ≤ 60 ms per frame for your module(s) on this machine. Cache static layers (draw once into an offscreen `Canvas`, copy rows into `c.d`).
4. Edit only your own files (named in your task) plus helpers `levels/<yours>-*.mjs` and tests `dev/<yours>-*.mjs`. Never edit `render.mjs`, `timeline.mjs`, `world.mjs`, `cute.mjs`, `levels/hero.mjs`, `levels/cta.mjs`, `levels/cards.mjs` or the other builder's files. If you need a change there, say so in your report (and work around it meanwhile).
5. No network, no paid APIs, no TTS, no `--video` renders, no ffmpeg encodes (the render slot is shared with other sessions). Stills and contact sheets are fine.
6. Copy code from earlier films freely, but never edit those folders: `../../gta/film` (street, sprite baking, cars, pedestrians, hero use), `../../cat-zoomies/film` (apartment, `person.mjs` — the recurring human hero in pyjamas + purple bobble hat), `../../touch/film` (the same hero outdoors), `../../plane/film/plane.mjs` (a cut-away jet), `../../cat-blink/film/catface.mjs` (big front-facing face), `../../cat-eyes/film` (cross-section panels), `../../netflix/film` and `../../minecraft/film` (city/game looks), `../../dog/shade.mjs` (volume-shading engine `drawPart`).
7. Sound: export `MOTIONS = [{ t0, t1, kind, level, note }]` from `levels/<yours>-motions.mjs`, computed from the same constants as the picture (every 8-bit sound lasts exactly as long as its motion). Kinds: whoosh, swish, click, blip, pop, sparkle, chime, zap, glitch, delete, engine, horn, step, chatter, tilt, dissolve, alert, uhoh, slap, boing, rise, fall, tap, squeak, thud, door, creak, wind, dash, hiss, bubble(s), puff, flip, tick, count, screen, glow, whistle, wrong. `level` = your module name. Keep it meaningful: one sound per visible motion, no constant noise.

## Checking your work
`node render.mjs --stills 2,9.5,13` writes `stills/tX.XX.png`; `node sheet.mjs dev/<yours>-sheet.jpg 2,9.5,13,16.5 4` makes a labelled contact sheet. **Look at them** (Read the images) and iterate until it is polished, the frame is full and every beat lands on its word. Also check a few in-between frames (motion, no pops). Time one frame (`node -e` importing `frame` from render.mjs, or time your module directly). Report: files, what is on screen when, times worth checking, frame timing, known issues, any change you need from the lead.
8. Catalog-ready assets (user decision 2026-10-28): draw every NEW reusable object as its own module `film/assets/<kind>.mjs` in the Kagenami catalog contract (`/Users/rv/Desktop/kagenami/packages/pixel-engine/catalog/items/README.md`: states, pure `draw(c, cx, cy, z, t, s)`, `{box, pt}`), driven in the film through a small `s` adapter. Do not write into the catalog yourself: the lead ports good assets after QA (`.agents/skills/kagenami-catalog/SKILL.md`). Never take art FROM the catalog (no backdrops, people, props or kits, even if a fitting kind exists): every film draws its own art from scratch in its own style (user, 2026-10-28).
