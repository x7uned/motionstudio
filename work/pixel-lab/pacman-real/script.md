## Narration (EN, read word for word), 214 words, ≈68–71 s

> In our world, Pac-Man would eat almost seven watermelons every second. Let's bring him here and check. Pac-Man has no official size, so let's make him as tall as a man, about 1.7 meters. In the arcade game, his body is about 13 pixels wide, and a dot is 2 pixels. So each dot is about 26 centimeters wide. That's a watermelon. On level one, Pac-Man eats at 25 kilometers an hour, with a tiny stop to swallow each dot. That's almost seven watermelons a second. One level has 240 dots. That's about two tons of food, heavier than a car. But eating slows him down. While he eats, the ghosts are faster. Later in the game, Blinky, the red ghost, gets even faster at the end of each level: 37 kilometers an hour. Pac-Man's top speed is 36. The big dots help less and less. On level one, the ghosts stay blue for six seconds. From level 19 on, they don't turn blue at all. So how does Pac-Man survive? Ghosts must reach the middle of a turn before they can turn. Pac-Man cuts the corner and wins a little distance every time. So Pac-Man's real superpower isn't eating. It's corners. Next, Sonic comes to our world. Follow, so you don't miss it.

## Picture: two worlds

**The game world (0 → «bring»).** Our drawing of a dark maze in the arcade spirit: blue double walls, dots, big dots, the ghost house. It is not a copy of the original maze. Pac-Man runs and eats, and two or three ghosts chase him. Everything is low-res sprites at an integer scale.

**Portal.** On «bring», a portal opens at the end of a corridor. Pac-Man dives into it, then a HARD CUT. In our city he flies out of the same portal.

**Our city.** A sunny summer street market at noon in a 3/4 view from above, full frame. This is different from Mario's evening street.
- Market stalls and a long line of watermelons one meter apart down the pavement, like dots.
- A crossroads with a sharp corner (for the finale).
- Passers-by at real scale, 1.6–1.85 m.

**Sprites.** Pac-Man is a game sprite: a yellow circle drawn as a pixel grid, with a mouth that opens and closes like in the game. Our drawing, not copied pixel for pixel. The ghosts (red, pink, blue, orange) are sprites too, with eyes that look where they go. Blue «frightened» ghosts and flashing come from the game. The city is detailed 32-bit pixel art; the contrast is the joke.

**Size.** Pac-Man is about 1.7 m, roughly the height of the passers-by, about 25% of the frame. There is one wide shot over the crossroads in the finale.

## Facts and calculations

| Fact | Value | Source / calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Speed at 100% | 75.76 px/s | Pac-Man Dossier, Table A.1 (pacman.holenet.info) |
| Level 1 speeds | Pac-Man 80%, while eating ~71%, ghosts 75% | Dossier, Table A.1 |
| Levels 5–20 | Pac-Man 100%, Elroy 2 (Blinky) 105% | Dossier, Table A.1 |
| Pause on a dot | 1 frame (1/60 s), big dot 3 frames | Dossier |
| Tile | 8×8 px, screen 28×36 tiles | Dossier |
| Dots | 240 small + 4 big = 244 | Dossier |
| Blue time | level 1: 6 s; levels 17, 19+: 0 | Dossier, table of frightened times |
| Cornering | Pac-Man turns before the middle of a turn, ghosts only at the middle | Dossier, «Cornering» |
| Pac-Man's size | ≈13 px across | **Not confirmed by a source.** This is the known size of the arcade sprite (in a 16×16 slot). Before the build, the builder checks it against the sprite they draw. In the text it is «about». |
| Dot | 2×2 px | same as above, «about» |

Calculations (scale: 13 px = 1.7 m → 1 px = 0.131 m):
- dot 2 px = 26 cm (a medium-large watermelon, 20–30 cm);
- tile 8 px = 1.05 m;
- level 1 while eating: 75.76 × 0.71 = 53.8 px/s = 7.0 m/s = **25.3 km/h**;
- dots per second: 53.8 / 8 = **6.7**. The same result comes from 80% speed plus a 1-frame stop on each dot, so the ~71% already includes the stops;
- ghosts on level 1: 75% = **26.7 km/h**, more than 25.3 while Pac-Man eats (but less than his 28.5 without dots);
- Pac-Man at 100% = **35.7 km/h**; Blinky (Elroy 2) at 105% = **37.4 km/h** (levels 5+, at the end of a level);
- 240 watermelons × ≈8 kg ≈ **1.9 t** («about two tons»). A 26 cm watermelon weighs 7–9 kg. A car is ≈1.3–1.5 t.

Assumptions:
- Pac-Man's height of 1.7 m is our choice, and we say so in the text;
- the dot weighs like a watermelon;
- the 13 px is not from a primary source (see the table).

Why the final line is true: the Dossier says Pac-Man does not have to wait for the middle of a turn, while ghosts do. That is the known reason why skilled players get away from ghosts through turns.
