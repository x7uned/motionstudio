## Film-specific notes
1. **breakout:**
   - Our own arcade cabinet in a dark arcade; its screen fills most of the frame. Field = 8 rows × 14 bricks, colour bands (bottom→top) yellow, green, orange, red, 2 rows each; points 1/3/5/7. No Atari logo or marks; on-screen labels in our fonts only.
   - Simulate the ball as a pure function of t (precomputed deterministic path, bounces exact): 4 paddle zones → 4 fixed directions regardless of incoming angle; speed steps on the 4th and 12th hit and a jump to top speed on the first orange/red brick; paddle shrinks to ½ on a breakout (ball through the red row hits the top wall), smoothly (≥6 frames), back to full width on the next serve. The ball rattling between the top wall and the wall's top after a breakout is the real look.
   - "Colours were fake": the four coloured strips peel off the glass (slide + dither) to show white bricks on a black-and-white tube, then return. Label «PLASTIC STRIPS».
   - Fast-forward for «clear the wall»: score counter 448 → second wall dissolves in → 896 MAX on an empty field. Keep it readable, eased.
   - Speed gauge with 4 steps (SLOW, 2, 3, TOP) and a HITS counter in the main safe zone.
2. **crt:**
   - Harness: copy the newest frozen harness `breakout/film` (render/captions, mix, synth-core, sheet, cover, cta, cute, tts.py, dev/qa.mjs, dev/pops.mjs).
   - One world: a 90s living room at evening (lamp light; you may copy the apartment look from `cat-zoomies/film`), a curved CRT TV on a stand with a console and a pad on the carpet. Later a flat modern screen slides in beside it (same room, no cut).
   - Inside the screens: OUR OWN level drawing (green checkered hills, palm-ish trees, a waterfall over a cliff, blue sky) and OUR OWN fast blue hedgehog runner — never Sega sprites, no logos. It should read as "that kind of game" without copying.
   - The CRT look must be computed, not faked: render the game picture at its own low resolution, then a CRT pass (horizontal blur/bloom between neighbours, dark gaps between lines, RGB triad mask on close-ups, slight glow). The flat screen shows the same picture as hard nearest-neighbour squares. Both are pure functions of t.
   - Key demo: the waterfall drawn as vertical stripes (water / background) — big zoom with labels WATER · BACK, then the same stripes through the CRT pass melt into see-through water with the hills visible behind. Side by side on the two screens later: CRT = clear water, flat = striped grid.
   - Close-up of the glass: a beam (bright dot with a short trail) paints line by line over red/green/blue phosphor dots; two neighbour pixels' glows overlap.
   - Quote card in our font: «WE MADE USE OF THE CRT'S BLURRINESS» — NAOTO OHSHIMA, SONIC DESIGNER (no face).
   - Checkerboard demo: two colours in a checkerboard → a third colour on the CRT; a checkerboard spotlight → soft light.
   - «CRT filter»: a toggle on the flat screen flips smoothly (dissolve) from hard squares to the CRT pass. Label «COLLECTORS» near the old TV (a small price-free tag).
   - Hook: frame 0 the hedgehog is already running on the CRT, camera already moving in.
3. **pal-50hz:**
   - Harness: copy the frozen harness `crt/film` (it already has the 90s living room, CRT TVs, our own hedgehog runner and hills level, console + pad). Reuse those drawings; never edit `crt/film`.
   - One world: two identical living rooms side by side, split by a thin wall, labels «USA · JAPAN» (left) and «EUROPE» (right) in our font (no flags). Each has a CRT, a console and a wall socket. Keep the TVs BIG (lesson from crt: avoid long dark or tiny close-ups; every demo must read at phone size).
   - Exact timing as a pure function of t: both screens run the same level; the left advances 60 game steps per second, the right 50 (game time = 5/6 of real time). The hedgehog, the music notes and the on-screen game clock all follow this. Frame 0: both already running, the right one visibly behind; the gap grows.
   - «electricity in the wall»: sine waves flow from each socket to its TV; counters «60 HZ» / «50 HZ».
   - «one step per picture»: a film strip of frames above each TV, each frame the hedgehog one step further; one-second bracket: 60 frames vs 50.
   - «best time … longer in real life»: both game clocks reach the same «0:59», while a wall clock (real time) above each shows more seconds on the right.
   - Black bars: on the right TV, bars dissolve in at top and bottom and the picture squashes a little (smooth).
   - «Most kids never knew»: the pyjama hero (`cat-zoomies/film/person.mjs`, read-only copy) plays happily in the Europe room.
   - Fixed vs not: a few cartridges get «PAL OK» ✓, others a grey «50 HZ» tag (no game names or logos).
   - Switch mod: a screwdriver, the console lid lifts on a hinge, a small 50/60 toggle goes in and clicks to 60 — the right hedgehog catches up smoothly.
   - Dreamcast: our own stylised white console labelled «1999» (no Sega logo), on-screen menu «60 HZ? YES».
   - Sound: the music bed may briefly demonstrate the effect (the Europe room's little tune at 5/6 tempo) if it stays clean under the voice.
