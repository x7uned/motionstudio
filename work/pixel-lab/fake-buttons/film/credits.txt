Sources:
- Crosswalk buttons: Michael Luo, "For Exercise in New York Futility, Push Button", The New York Times, 2004-02-27 — city DOT
  figures: more than 2,500 of the 3,250 walk buttons no longer did anything after computer-controlled signals took over.
- Elevator close button: 2010 ADA Standards / ADA Accessibility Guidelines 4.10 (minimum door-open times; the door-close
  button cannot shorten them); fire-service mode requires the door-close button (ASME A17.1). In many elevators the button
  works after the minimum time; the film says "for the first few seconds".
- Dummy thermostats: ACHR News, "Placebo Stats", 2003-03-31 — informal web poll: 51 of 70 respondents had installed dummy
  thermostats.
- Phone camera: smartphone cameras use electronic shutters; the click is a played sound.
- Illusion of control: Ellen J. Langer, "The Illusion of Control", Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 1975.
