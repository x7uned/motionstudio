Is my dog blind? It can't even find a red ball on green grass. Not quite. Your dog can see the ball. It just can't see red. Your eyes have three kinds of color cells: for red, green and blue. A dog's eyes have only two: one for blue, one for yellow. So for a dog, red and green look almost the same: a dull, yellowish brown. That red ball is the same color as the grass. While it flies, your dog can follow it. But once it stops, it gets lost in the grass. Dogs also see less detail. What you see clearly across the park, a dog has to get almost four times closer to see. So your dog does what it does best: it uses its nose. Want an easy fix? Get a blue or yellow ball.
